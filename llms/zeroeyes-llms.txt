# ZeroEyes

> ZeroEyes is an AI-powered visual firearm detection platform that uses existing security cameras and human verification to identify guns and reduce response time.

This site provides product and technology information, industry solutions, gun violence research, resources, partnership programs, and funding information. Use the sections below to find detailed pages on each topic.

## Product & technology

- [Home](https://zeroeyes.com/): ZeroEyes overview and main product information
- [Technology](https://zeroeyes.com/technology): Visual firearm detection technology, AI and human verification
- [3D site mapping](https://zeroeyes.com/3d-site-mapping): Drone and LiDAR 3D mapping for campuses and facilities to support safety planning and responder navigation
- [Request a demo](https://zeroeyes.com/request-a-demo): Schedule a demo or get in touch with sales
- [Pricing](https://zeroeyes.com/pricing): Per-camera pricing and assessment
